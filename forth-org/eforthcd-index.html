Index of eForth Distribution CD


This is a collection of Forth programs and Forth hardware designs placed in
the public domain by the eForth Group to encourage hardware and software 
engineers to study and evaluate computers and software based on the eForth model.
Due to the limited time available, this collection is an alpha release,
which collects the existing materials in a somewhat organized manner. The 
materials included, especially the tutorials, will be revised to make the
presentations clearer and more easily understandable.

Comments and criticism are welcome. A web site will be available to release
future editions and to receive user feedback, http://www.eforth.com.tw.
